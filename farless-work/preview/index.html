    Questions

    Good to know.

    Everything you need to know about your Farless 5-in-1 Tap.

    What makes Farless different?

    Five waters from one beautifully made tap: hot, cold, filtered, boiling and sparkling.

    From washing up to your favourite drinks, Farless makes the kitchen simpler without giving up an ounce of style.

    What comes with my Farless?

    Your Farless 5-in-1 Tap, plus everything it needs to pour boiling and sparkling water.

    Filtered water runs through a separate filtration system, which isn't included.

    Can I use my own water filter?

    Yes. Farless connects to a separate filtration system, so the choice of filter is yours.

    Compatibility depends on your filter's specifications and connections, so please check before you buy.

    Do I need Farless replacement filters?

    No. There are no Farless-only filters to buy.

    Choose a compatible system from the brand you trust, and keep to the maintenance schedule that suits your home.

    How clean is the filtered water?

    That depends on the filtration system you choose, as each one is designed to reduce different contaminants.

    We recommend a certified system that matches your household's water.

    Can I adjust the boiling water temperature?

    Yes. Adjustable temperature settings let you pour exactly what you need, from tea and coffee to water for cooking.

    Is boiling water safe with children around?

    Safety is built in. Boiling water only flows once the pin is pressed and the handle turned, and a built-in child-safety lock helps prevent it starting by accident.

    We still recommend an adult nearby whenever very hot water is in use.

    Can it pour chilled sparkling water?

    Yes. Choose chilled or room-temperature sparkling water, whenever the mood strikes.

    How does the sparkling water work?

    A carbonation system with a replaceable CO₂ cylinder adds the bubbles right at your tap.

    When the cylinder runs out, swap in a new one and carry on.

    Is it still an everyday kitchen tap?

    Absolutely. Hot and cold water work just as you'd expect, for washing up, rinsing vegetables and cleaning.

    The extra waters add convenience and take nothing away.

    Do I replace the whole tap when the CO₂ runs out?

    No. Only the CO₂ cylinder needs replacing. Your tap stays exactly where it is.

    What if something goes wrong?

    Every Farless comes with a 5-year warranty.
